Platform Overview

How It Works

Three intelligent steps turn raw customer behaviour into revenue-protecting decisions.

01
Data Ingestion

Input Customer Behaviour

Enter key RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) metrics for any customer alongside behavioural signals like category diversity and purchase count. The form accepts individual data entry or bulk JSON import, making it flexible for both quick spot-checks and batch analysis workflows.

  • Days since last purchase (Recency)
  • Total number of orders (Frequency)
  • Total lifetime spend (Monetary value)
  • Product category spread & diversity score
02
Machine Learning

AI Analyses Hidden Patterns

The moment you submit, NexIQ's Logistic Regression pipeline — trained on thousands of real Olist e-commerce transactions — scores every signal against learned drop-off patterns. The model normalises inputs, weights each feature, and outputs a calibrated probability within milliseconds.

  • Pre-trained sklearn pipeline with StandardScaler
  • Calibrated probability output (0 – 100 %)
  • Instant, server-side inference with no latency
  • Feature importance traced back to your inputs
03
Actionable Insights

Get Risk Score + Retention Actions

Results are returned as a clear risk tier — Low, Medium, or High — with a visual gauge, a health score bar, and AI-generated recommendations tailored to the customer's specific behavioural profile. Every prediction is persisted to the dashboard for trend analysis over time.

  • Visual churn-risk gauge (0 – 100 %)
  • Colour-coded risk badge (Low / Medium / High)
  • Personalised re-engagement action list
  • All predictions stored for dashboard analytics

Getting Started

How to Use It

Follow these four steps to go from raw customer data to a clear, actionable churn decision.

①

Navigate to the Predict Page

Click Predict in the top navigation bar or hit the Start Analysis button on this page. You will land on the prediction form where you can enter a single customer's details.

②

Fill in the Customer Fields

Enter the required fields: Recency (days since last order), Frequency (total number of orders), Monetary Value (total lifetime spend in BRL), Category Diversity (unique product categories purchased), and Purchase Count. All fields are validated in real time — red borders indicate invalid input, green borders confirm correct data.

💡 Tip: Use a Recency of 90–120+ to simulate dormant customers who are most likely to churn.

③

Submit & Read the Risk Output

Press Run Prediction. The right panel will animate and reveal:

  • Risk Gauge: A circular meter showing churn probability as a percentage.
  • Risk Badge: Colour-coded label — green (Low), amber (Medium), red (High).
  • Health Score Bar: An inverse measure of customer retention health.
  • Key Insight: A plain-language summary of what the score means.
  • Recommended Actions: Specific steps your team can take to retain this customer.
④

Monitor Trends on the Dashboard

Every prediction you run is automatically saved. Head to the Dashboard to see aggregate KPIs, a risk-distribution donut chart, a recency trend line, and a searchable table of all historical predictions. Use the filter controls to isolate High-risk customers and export the data as CSV for your CRM team.

💡 Tip: Run 10+ predictions to populate the trend charts and unlock the full power of the analytics view.
