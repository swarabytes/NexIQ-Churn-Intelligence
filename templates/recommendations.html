Retention Playbook

Use these categorized playbooks to proactively address churn risks based on customer priority levels.

Financial Offers

Best for customers with High Monthly Charges and high probability.
  • Offer 15% discount for 12-month contract upgrade.
  • Waive fiber-optic installation or equipment fees.
  • Provide a temporary credit for long-term loyalists.

Engagement & Value

Best for customers with Short Tenure (< 12 months) and Month-to-month plans.
  • Trigger "Success Milestone" email at Month 6.
  • Personalized tutorial on high-impact service features.
  • Invite to beta-testing group for new service rollouts.

Proactive Support

Best for "Critical" priority customers with Fiber Optic service.
  • Priority concierge support for next 3 months.
  • Scheduled "Health Check" call with Account Manager.
  • Free technical audit of existing home setup.

Retention Tip: The "Why" Matters

Model insights show that Contract Duration is the single biggest predictor of churn. Focus your highest effort on converting Month-to-month customers to annual plans, even if it requires a significant initial discount.
